Our Mission & Story

Built by Students, Designed for Students

CampusCart was created to solve a real everyday college problem: getting high-quality, dependable study essentials without overpriced bookstore markups.

Why CampusCart Was Created

Every semester, college students face the exact same scramble: searching across multiple city shops for durable backpacks, smooth non-smearing exam pens, notebooks with paper that won't bleed through highlighters, and study lights that don't disturb sleeping dorm roommates.

Local campus convenience stores often charge inflated retail prices, while big e-commerce sites ship slowly with steep delivery fees on single small items. CampusCart bridges this gap by offering a curated catalog of academic essentials tailored to student budgets with fast, direct-to-campus delivery.
